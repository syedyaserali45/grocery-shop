Our story

The neighbourhood grocery shop, now online

FreshMart started in 2014 as a small corner store in Gachibowli. Today we serve thousands of families across Hyderabad — with the same promise we began with: fresh produce, fair prices and a smile with every order.

  • Farm-fresh produce sourced every morning from local farmers
  • Strict quality and hygiene checks on every single item
  • Easy, no-questions-asked returns within 7 days
  • Secure payments — UPI, cards and cash on delivery
